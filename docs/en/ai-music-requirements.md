# AI Music: Source Transparency and Informed Choice

Product requirements | 2026-11-01

Help listeners understand available production information before listening, saving or purchasing, and provide controls, correction requests and traceable outcomes.

## Evidence

Public user feedback, reporting and platform materials inform the problem; client behavior, detector performance and review capacity require validation.

## Verification

Record production roles and evidence. Use basic checks, asynchronous screening and targeted rechecks, with human review for unresolved conflicts, appeals and audits.

## Source and preference rules

Separate verified generation, publisher declarations, active suspicions and unknowns. A matching generated component excludes the track; a human contribution cannot override other generated components.

## Purchases and corrections

Apply explicit source and license admission rules, disclose price and rights, preserve order snapshots and link material corrections to affected purchases.

## Deliverables

A focused Chinese PRD, listener wireframes, publisher operations, phased acceptance criteria and capacity planning. Validate outcomes through task tests and implementation review.

## Operating rules

- Store evidence by production component. Preserve publisher declarations separately from verified findings.
- Active generation suspicions are independent of case progress. Missing material alone neither proves generation nor cancels an existing valid suspicion.
- A verified human contribution does not override generated or suspected content in another component.
- For new pilot purchases, complete and unconflicted declarations may be admitted after required checks, with unverified scope disclosed. An unresolved relevant suspicion, unknown required component, failed license check or incomplete check pauses admission.
- Preserve existing purchased rights and order disclosure snapshots. Review corrections and remedies against the affected transaction.
- Use source-bearing evidence, calibrated screening, targeted checks, appeals and negative-sample audits. Detector scores are not percentages of AI contribution.

## Verification and acceptance

The PRD specifies 24 phased acceptance conditions. Current-client behavior, detector applicability, review capacity and user understanding are evaluated before expansion. Strict verified-source recommendations and pricing experiments have separate entry conditions.
